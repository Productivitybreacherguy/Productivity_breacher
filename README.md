# Productivity_breacher
This app is made for MacOS users that struggle with playing games during breaks and making those breaks... a little too long. it's still in beta, but it's usable. set your timer (10s,20m,1h or so) and play!when it runs out, your gaming apps such as roblox or discord are temporarily locked until you set the next timer. closes with ESC. enjoy!
when you install the program, you will need to transfer it to the applications folder. if you try to open it and it says "Apple could not verify “Productivity Breacher” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy." press "Done." then,open system settings, go into the Privacy & Security folder, scroll down and when you see ""Productivity breacher" was blocked to protect your Mac." press "Open Anyway". This will open the app, but you should know that it's still in beta, so since you can't close It with "Quit" I left several backup options such as ESC, force quit through option+command+esc. enjoy!
