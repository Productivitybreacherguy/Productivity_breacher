# Productivity_breacher
This app is made for MacOS users that struggle with playing games during breaks and making those breaks... a little too long. it's still in beta, but it's usable. set your timer (10s,20m,1h or so) and play!when it runs out, your gaming apps such as roblox or discord are temporarily locked until you set the next timer. closes with ESC. enjoy!
when you install the program, you will need to transfer it to the applications folder. if you try to open it and it says that something went wrong bcuz apple doesn't know the creator blah blah blah just open your settings go into the confidentiality and safety folder and scroll down till you see the open anyway or whatever button (just know that im not American and I dont exactly know the names of the folders so I guess trust yourself on this one :-)
